Q4: How Exactly Will We Execute?

Specialized Roles & Predictive Methodologies

Roles: Hunters, Farmers and Sales Operations

🏹
Hunters

Focus: New logo acquisition, initial 'big ticket' orders (100-500 laptops).

Awarded on onboarding client & first order. Drives 70-80% of New Revenue.

Responsible for lead gen, networking, prospecting, and developing new avenues (projectors, displays etc.).

🧑‍🌾
Farmers

Focus: Client growth & retention, maximizing potential (average 500 laptops/client).

Awarded on upsell/cross-sell & client retention. Ensures High NRR & LTV.

Ensures low rental inventory, interfaces with CRM for smooth execution & customer service.

📊
Sales Operations

Provides critical support for Hunters and Farmers in MIS, database management, and analytics. Does not directly acquire or grow business.

Predictive Sales Methodologies

BANT for Hunting 🏹

Qualify leads for Budget, Authority, Need, and Timeline to ensure maximum efficiency.

SPIN for Farming 🧑‍🌾

Uncover deeper needs by discussing Situation, Problem, Implication, and Need-Payoff.
